02 / How we work Three disciplines
001

Look development

Materials, light and camera resolved in the engine, at final quality, while the decision is still cheap to change. What you approve is what renders.

002

Real-time delivery

Sequences that run at frame rate on the hardware they ship to — from a browser canvas to a stage LED wall — without a second offline pipeline shadowing the first.

003

Interactive systems

When the image has to answer to something — a visitor, a data feed, a live performance — we build the system underneath it, not just the render on top.
